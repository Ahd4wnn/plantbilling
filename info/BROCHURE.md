# Plantora — Brochure Copy (ready to lay out)

Ready-to-use copy for a printed/shareable brochure. Nothing here needs rewriting —
drop each block into Canva, Figma, or a print template as-is. Two formats are provided:

1. **Tri-fold** (6 panels, A4 folded in three) — the main deliverable.
2. **One-pager / flyer** (single page) — for a quick handout or a PDF on WhatsApp.

> **Brand:** product = **Plantora** (a.k.a. PlantBill), by **Dofida Private Limited**.
> Lead with Plantora; place a small *"A Dofida product"* line + the `info/logo.png`
> monogram in a footer/corner. Colors: botanical green `#2E7D46`, warm off-white
> `#F7F5EF`, near-black text `#1A1A1A`. Bold legible sans-serif, generous whitespace.
> Currency always ₹, 2 decimals. Audience: Indian plant-shop owners, often elderly —
> keep it warm, plain, and reassuring.

## FORMAT 1 — TRI-FOLD (6 panels)

A tri-fold has two sides. When folded, the reader sees the **Front cover** first,
opens to the **inside spread** (3 panels), and the **back** carries contact/close.

Panel order when printed:
- **Outside sheet:** [ Back panel | Front flap | **Front cover** ]
- **Inside sheet:** [ Inside 1 | Inside 2 | Inside 3 ]

## Layout & print notes (for whoever designs it)

- **Tri-fold size:** A4 (297×210 mm) landscape, folded in three → 99 mm panels. Add
  **3–5 mm bleed** on every outer edge and keep text **≥ 8–10 mm** from folds/trim.
- **Panel widths differ slightly** on a real tri-fold (the folded-in flap is ~2 mm
  narrower). Use a proper tri-fold template rather than three equal columns.
- **Type sizes:** headlines 28–40 pt, section titles 14–16 pt (tracked caps), body
  10.5–12 pt with generous line-height. Never go below 9 pt — the audience is elderly.
- **Colour:** botanical green `#2E7D46` for headings/accents, near-black `#1A1A1A` for
  body on warm off-white `#F7F5EF`. High contrast; avoid grey-on-grey.
- **Imagery:** reuse the real app screenshots in `info/ads/` for authentic phone
  mockups; keep one consistent phone frame and lighting across panels.
- **Logo:** always composite the real `info/logo.png` monogram; never let a tool redraw it.
- **Export:** CMYK PDF/X-1a at 300 dpi for print; a separate RGB PDF for WhatsApp/web.
- **Fill the blanks:** every `__________` (phone, email, address) must be replaced with
  real contact details before printing.
